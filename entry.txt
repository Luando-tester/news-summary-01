Ukraine-Russia-US talks in Abu Dhabi agreed on a major prisoner exchange, possibly about 1,500 prisoners from each side, though precise terms remained tentative.

Secretary of State Marco Rubio said economic pressure meant Washington did not need a major campaign against Iran. Tehran kept Hormuz closed; the IRGC said a tanker struck a naval mine, amid 37 reported November shipping attacks.

Congo's Ebola outbreak reached 11,042 cases and 5,387 deaths, the second largest in history. Kenya discharged its last Ebola patient and began the 42-day countdown to declaring its outbreak over.

At COP31 in Antalya, UN Secretary-General António Guterres warned the world was on a path to destruction. He urged faster emissions cuts.

Chinese defence-linked universities obtained two restricted Nvidia H200 chips. The board opened an investigation after reports linked CEO Jensen Huang to a woman accused of spying for China; shares fell about 10% in two sessions.

The US House voted 211-204 to refer the impeachment resolution against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to committee, effectively burying it.

Indonesian Vice President Gibran resigned after 120 lawmakers from eight parties backed impeachment. A court had voided his education certificate.

Post-Diwali smog sent New Delhi pollution readings beyond the scale, at 66 times the WHO daily guideline. Officials denied hiding the data.

Israeli fire killed several people in Gaza, including a father and his 13-year-old son, according to Gaza health officials. The deaths tested the ceasefire.

Wang Yi called a phone conversation with Rubio “positive and constructive” ahead of the APEC summit in Shenzhen. Trump and Xi are expected there; a Trump-Xi-Putin trilateral is under consideration.
